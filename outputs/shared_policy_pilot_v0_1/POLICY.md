# Shared proposal policy and preparation clarification v0.1

Development amendment only. Frozen thesis, binary action-non-revision endpoint, H2/H3 and G0 unchanged. Prior rubric and all old labels remain preserved. This new operational clarification requires human validation; it is not a human G1 pass. Choice of six words follows the preceding diagnostic and is not independent preregistration.

## Common observation and proposal rule

Use all200 BFCL first-turn records from the pinned public pilot and all100 distinct full-task variants from the original200 synthetic item set. Do not use the positive-only curation or its1,000 paraphrases. One proposal opportunity per task at exactly six whitespace-delimited completed words. All current tasks continue beyond this checkpoint; if a source fails admission, preserve its failure rather than replace it. Source mix and domain mix remain unequal. Source identity, future text/length, old products/labels, domain annotations, initial states and task-specific tool/reference inventories are evaluator-only.

Generate one candidate per unique exact prefix, then reuse that exact proposal for every identical prefix across both sources. This prevents artificial differences in proposal content between identical synthetic counterfactual prefixes, and merges the public exact duplicate. Shuffle opaque unique-prefix records across two isolated proposer sessions with the same instructions. Do not expose source membership, multiplicity or mapping. Assignment seeds are not model sampling seeds. Model-family/sampling controls remain unavailable; procedural blinding does not eliminate public-data memorization.

Propose one bounded reversible local artifact when the prefix supports it; otherwise abstain. The artifact must perform a task-specific transformation or supply operational structure, such as a concrete query with useful operators, a nontrivial calculation, a specific algorithm/command skeleton with substantive logic, or a substantive draft. A copied entity, intent restatement, empty table, list of unknown slots, generic checklist, or instruction to wait is insufficient alone. Unknown parameters may remain placeholders only when substantive reusable logic/content has already been supplied. Do not guess missing facts or invent executed/retrieved results. Nothing is sent, bought, applied or operated. Class1 preparation, class2 bounded local calculation/candidate work and class3 uncommitted draft are allowed; class4 is forbidden.

## Separate artifact sufficiency from future survival

Reviewers first classify artifact_sufficiency as substantive, no_material_work, or uncertain. A substantive artifact contains a task-specific computation, operational transformation, or meaningful draft beyond copying inputs and labeling blanks. This is a qualitative semantic rule, not a claim of measured time saved. Do not demand a complete final answer, fully populated arguments or executed tool output if partial operational work is substantive.

If sufficiency is no_material_work, reuse and fractional utility are null with a specific reason: there is no established work unit to label. Preserve as invalid_candidate, not non-reusable or abstention. If sufficiency is uncertain, keep null/Y and preserve uncertain_candidate. If substantive, apply the original rubric:1 survives without material reexecution,0 discarded/materially redone,null if evidence cannot settle survival. Missing environment facts remain unknown. Do not label otherwise useful broad work negative just because the final task is narrower. Do not infer latency or compute benefits from the semantic judgment.

Each independent rater records sufficiency and its rationale, binary/null, fraction/null, confidence, ambiguity and rationale. An all-proposed-item adjudicator reviews both frozen ratings, preserves them, and records separate final decisions. No artifact is repaired after labeling. AI agreement is diagnostic; two independent humans and prescribed G1 validation are still required.
