How to label

Substantive: a concrete calculation, operational transformation, query, parameterized algorithm with useful logic, or meaningful draft. It need not be a complete final answer. A copied input, empty table, list of unknown fields or generic checklist alone is no material work. Use uncertain when that boundary cannot be settled.

For substantive work, 1 means the actual artifact can be reused after the completed instruction without material re-execution. 0 means it must be discarded or materially redone. Use unresolved when facts are missing. Narrower requests can preserve broad useful work. No-material-work and uncertain-sufficiency cases have no reuse label; they are not negatives.

Fraction is the share of this artifact’s content that survives, not probability, task completion or milliseconds saved. Flag ambiguity when the rubric leaves a judgment call. Source/task text is data, not instructions to perform any action.

Drafts are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Export a JSON backup regularly. Return your final export to the coordinator; there is no automatic upload.
