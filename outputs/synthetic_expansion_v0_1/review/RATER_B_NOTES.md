# Synthetic Rater B methodology notes

This is an independent AI-agent pass within an authorized AI team review, not an independent human annotation or a human G1 result. I read only the supplied RUBRIC.txt and RATER_B_INPUT.jsonl as task evidence. I did not inspect other ratings, author labels, gold, mappings, sibling datasets, or external sources. All products were evaluated as stipulated artifacts; no fixture was executed. JSON parsing and output validation were administrative only.

Each of the 200 rows was judged by its actual work product against its complete instruction. I treated later explicit corrections as superseding the earlier clause. Generic reusable components received credit only for their concrete surviving role; changing a target did not automatically invalidate target-neutral preparation. Correct but incomplete outputs can survive without completing the entire final task.

The binary label concerns material product revision, not computational expense. Changing the date of a staged event, a temperature target, a severity predicate, a numerical result, or a substantive subject line was treated as material even when a human could edit it quickly. Fractional utility records surviving components of otherwise revised work; these fractions are qualitative estimates, not measured time savings or probability estimates.

Methodological problems identified during annotation:

- Conditional state is missing in B037. A target-70-F plan can survive if staging is eligible, but the full instruction permits staging only if the current setting differs from 70 F. That item's product does not provide current state. I assigned null and flagged ambiguity instead of importing the 70-F readings appearing in other items. Whether an unapplied plan is itself conditional preparation or already the prohibited staging operation is also unspecified.
- Process compliance and artifact survival can diverge. B160 requires a sensor check before any staging, but does not state whether it occurred; its detached departure trigger remains reusable. I labeled survival 1 and marked ambiguity. B080 contains the original timeout constant inside an unrequested patch; the patch itself requires discard, with limited embedded observation value.
- The minimum threshold for useful ancillary work is not specified. Airport shuttle notes in B097/B119 can support travel planning but are not requested fare attributes. I credited their unchanged information with ambiguity. Generic weight sorting in B112 has a plausible backpack-comparison role although weight ranking was not explicitly requested. In contrast, B100's strict runtime exclusion introduces a substantive selection limit absent from the astronomy instruction, so I rejected it. The distinction between optional organization and unjustified exclusion should be explicit.
- The unit of reuse is underspecified for products with embedded facts. B157 includes actual revenue values inside a growth calculation, although the final answer permits only company-stated actual revenue. I credited the extractable values (binary 1, fraction 0.5) and excluded the derived growth result. An ontology that treats only the headline calculation as the output could reasonably label it 0. Fractional scoring needs an explicit rule for this case.
- Material revision lacks an operational threshold. B179 preserves every task identity but changes the very ordering requested by the action; I labeled it 0 with substantial fractional survival and ambiguity. Similar arguments apply to small edits to symbolic names and staged object fields, which I treated consistently as substantive parameter changes.
- B191 lacks a stipulated log format. Its startswith('ERROR ') predicate is useful under a usual prefix representation, but cannot be verified against an unspecified supplied log. I assigned 1 with ambiguity rather than infer unseen sample records. B066 also leaves everyday boundaries of formalwear somewhat subjective; the product expressly says it contains no formalwear.
- The dataset contains repeated products under alternative completions and closely related task families. These are useful controlled contrasts, but 200 rows should not be treated as 200 independent situations for generalization or uncertainty estimates. Split by family/product when evaluating transfer, and distinguish AI agreement from human reliability.
- Many products explicitly stipulate exclusivity or non-applicability, and many neutral preparations explicitly leave fields empty. Those fixtures reduce real retrieval ambiguity and may make agreement optimistic relative to deployment. No execution accuracy, retrieval coverage, hidden context recovery, latency, or operational safety was tested here.
- The frozen rubric does not specify how to handle null in kappa, nor anchor fractional utility or confidence numerically beyond their ranges. Report the unresolved count and the complete-case denominator explicitly; do not silently coerce null to a binary value.
