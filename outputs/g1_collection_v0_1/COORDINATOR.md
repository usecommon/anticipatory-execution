Each ZIP contains an exact copy of the reviewed blank workbook, the original rubric and role-specific instructions. No gold key is packaged. The two workbooks deliberately retain the established sheet structure; each person fills only their named sheet.

Compute agreement before adjudication. Have a distinct third adjudicator resolve disagreements with item ID, both original labels, final judgment and reason. Preserve original judgments. Closure requires a separate evidence-backed report, actual independence and adjudication records, valid labels and the prescribed reliability criterion. Do not mark G1 passed from workbook appearance, generated labels or the owner key. This package changes no thesis, gate or protocol and authorizes no extraction.
